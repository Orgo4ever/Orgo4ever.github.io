Power BI · DAX · Financial Analytics · Data Modeling

Revenue & Margin Intelligence

Where is revenue missing its budget, and which products contribute most to profitability?

Built two interactive Power BI dashboards using Microsoft AdventureWorksDW sample data. Modeled separate financial and sales reporting tables, created reusable DAX measures, and added reporting-period, revenue-account and product filters for self-service analysis.

Financial performance: Actual revenue of $16.88M versus an $18.66M budget represented a 9.52% shortfall, with approximately 97% of the gap concentrated in Trade Sales.

Commercial profitability: The separate sales analysis covered 29.31M in recorded sales and a 41.13% gross margin, with trends and product-level comparisons supporting further investigation.

Documented currency filters, matched budget dates and other reporting limitations so the findings remain interpretable and are not mistaken for a complete company financial statement.

Vanguard Digital Journey A/B Test

Did a redesigned digital customer journey improve valid completion enough to justify further investment?

In a collaborative Ironhack analytics project, we analyzed event-level digital interactions, reconstructed ordered customer journeys, and evaluated a redesigned Test experience against the existing Control experience. The workflow combined Python analysis, statistical testing, and Tableau reporting.

My contributions focused on building the data foundation, demographic analysis, defining valid completion, calculating uplift, and translating performance into business-facing metrics.

Experiment result: Valid completion improved from 45.98% in Control to 48.10% in Test: a statistically significant 2.12-percentage-point gain (approximately 4.6% relative lift).

Business interpretation: The gain fell just short of the predefined 5% relative-lift target. Median journey duration also increased from approximately 160 to 168 seconds, suggesting a trade-off between completion and speed.

Recommendation: Retain promising redesign elements, investigate repeated interactions and remaining drop-off, and run a follow-up controlled test before declaring the experience fully optimized.

UK Pharmaceutical Procurement Analytics

How reliably can procurement activity be understood when award counts are highly concentrated and financial reporting is incomplete?

Working with a supplied database schema, I developed MySQL analysis and interactive Power BI and Tableau reports covering 170 procurement processes and 1,222 awards in a 2024 UK procurement extract.

In Power BI, I prepared four tables with Power Query, configured relationships and built DAX measures for procurement activity, award concentration and contract data quality. I checked report totals against SQL results and verified procurement-method filtering.

Just 12 procurement processes account for 69.56% of award records. Missing or nominal £1 values affect 65.05% of contract records, making reported values unreliable for expenditure comparisons. The reports emphasize participation, award distribution and transparent data-quality limitations.

Is Europe's accelerating AI adoption developing alongside energy conditions that could support sustainable growth?

Predicting Concrete Compressive Strength

Can material composition and curing age provide useful preliminary strength estimates?

Developed a supervised regression workflow using 1,005 observations, domain-informed features, ensemble methods and cross-validation. The final Gradient Boosting model achieved a held-out test MAE of 2.66 MPa and R² of 0.941.

The model supports preliminary screening, not replacement of physical material testing. Validation decisions and limitations are documented in the repository.
