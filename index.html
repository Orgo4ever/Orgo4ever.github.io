SQL · Power BI · Power Query · DAX · Tableau

UK Pharmaceutical Procurement Analytics

How reliably can procurement activity be understood when award counts are highly concentrated and financial reporting is incomplete?

Working with a supplied database schema, I developed MySQL analysis and interactive Power BI and Tableau reports covering 170 procurement processes and 1,222 awards in a 2024 UK procurement extract.

In Power BI, I prepared four tables with Power Query, configured relationships and built DAX measures for procurement activity, award concentration and contract data quality. I checked report totals against SQL results and verified procurement-method filtering.

Just 12 procurement processes account for 69.56% of award records. Missing or nominal £1 values affect 65.05% of contract records, making reported values unreliable for expenditure comparisons. The reports emphasize participation, award distribution and transparent data-quality limitations.

GrantScope — Research Funding Discovery

How can researchers connect new ideas to historically funded research and funding trends?

GrantScope combines an interactive Python and Streamlit application with a Tableau funding analytics dashboard. The application helps users discover historically funded research related to their ideas through concept similarity ranking, research data integration, and interactive filters.

The complementary Tableau dashboard enables exploration of the broader research funding landscape, connecting individual project discovery with funding trends and patterns across research programs.

Explore the project: Try the live application to investigate a research concept, or open the Tableau dashboard to explore the funding landscape. Similarity scores indicate relevance, not funding probability.

Python · Pandas · Data Integration · EDA

European AI Growth & Environmental Sustainability

Is Europe's accelerating AI adoption developing alongside energy conditions that could support sustainable growth?

In this collaborative Ironhack project, I developed the primary Python analytical workflow, integrating Eurostat AI-adoption, renewable-energy, and emissions data with PeeringDB data-centre records.

Using pandas, I standardized country and year identifiers, merged datasets, investigated cross-country relationships, and created comparative visualizations. The findings highlighted that AI adoption alone is not a reliable indicator of environmental sustainability.

My focus: Data acquisition, cleaning, cross-source integration, exploratory analysis, and responsible interpretation of statistical associations. This was a team project, with individual contributions documented in the repository.
